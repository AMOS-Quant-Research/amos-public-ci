# AMOS research boundary

1. Natural-language intake produces structured, auditable hypotheses.
2. Potential Analyzer checks research readiness only, before downstream AI agents.
3. Potential does not estimate measured PF, EV, win rate, correlation or live risk from descriptions.
4. Native NautilusTrader simulation with real Bid/Ask QuoteTicks owns performance measurement.
5. No live trading, broker execution, or order permissions in this module.
6. Never bypass validation due to a favorable preflight result. Missing evidence is reported, not invented.
